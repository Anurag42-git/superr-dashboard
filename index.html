The Pressure Index in one line

Index = 100 × Stage × (½ Closeness + ½ Squeeze) in a chase, and 100 × Stage × Squeeze in the first innings. Stage grows through the innings, Closeness is how even the win probability is, and Squeeze combines the required rate (or being behind par) with wickets lost.

What we checked

Read this before quoting a rank

Pressure results come from about 120–550 balls per player, and single balls are very noisy. Two random halves of the data give only weakly similar orderings (correlation for batters, for bowlers), so the top 10 is the best-supported group, and the exact order inside it is less certain than the table suggests. The “top-10 chance” on every player is the honest measure of that.

Bye and leg-bye runs are approximated, and opposition strength has two levels only. Full details are in the methodology report.
